Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 04, 2026

At AeroClick, we prioritize the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outline the types of information we collect and how we safeguard it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not request personal identity details like email addresses or phone numbers during our username-only sign up process. We collect:

  • Your chosen Username and join timestamps.
  • Training results: Game scores, Click Speeds (CPS), Accuracy percentages, and Response speeds.
  • Match History Logs.

2. How We Use Your Data

We utilize the collected information to generate global leaderboard standing statistics, track achievements, and display personal progress metrics inside your dashboard.

3. Third-Party Integrations

We use Google Firebase Services as our secure cloud backend. Data submitted is stored securely in Firebase Auth and Firestore. We do not sell or trade user data to third-party brokers.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 04, 2026

AeroClick utilizes local browser caches to optimize performance and save user preferences.

1. How We Use Cookies

We use session tokens, local storage variables, and basic cached packages to:

  • Maintain user authentication sessions.
  • Store setup configurations and active theme preferences.
  • Run local offline simulations if cloud services are unavailable.

2. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can manage or clear cached cookie histories directly within your browser settings. However, doing so will clear offline local storage profiles.
